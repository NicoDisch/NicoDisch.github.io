Must Read for Flow Matching and Diffusion

Papers and books I’d hand to a colleague, each with a note on why.

Generative modeling

  • Flow Matching for Generative Modeling Lipman et al., ICLR 2023
    Regress a vector field onto simple conditional paths. Training ends up as boring as supervised learning, which is the point. With straight paths the whole loss fits on one line: $$\mathcal{L}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{t,\,x_0,\,x_1}\big\| v_\theta(x_t, t) - (x_1 - x_0) \big\|^2, \quad x_t = (1-t)\,x_0 + t\,x_1$$
  • Flow Straight and Fast: Rectified Flow Liu, Gong, Liu, ICLR 2023
    Same idea from another angle, plus reflow to straighten paths for few-step sampling.
  • Building Normalizing Flows with Stochastic Interpolants Albergo, Vanden-Eijnden, ICLR 2023
    The general frame the two above fit into.
  • Score-Based Generative Modeling through SDEs Song et al., ICLR 2021
    Diffusion as an SDE, with the probability flow ODE on the side. Read this first if diffusion still feels like magic.
  • Neural Ordinary Differential Equations Chen et al., NeurIPS 2018
    Where continuous-time deep learning starts.
